ThunderSub issues from user feedback
====================================

1. Scanning large Gmail accounts is too slow
   A mailbox with roughly 40,000 messages took more than 10 hours to scan.
   Investigate remote-message network round trips, offline synchronization,
   concurrency, and per-folder timing diagnostics.

2. Legitimate correspondents are suggested for unsubscribe
   Senders the user has previously emailed can appear as subscriptions.
   Investigate scanning Sent mail for correspondent signals and providing an
   opt-in "trust people I have emailed" filter without hiding real mailing lists.

3. Some jobs hang indefinitely
   Unsubscribe or cleanup jobs can remain active until Thunderbird is restarted.
   Add operation timeouts and enough stage/timing information to identify where
   a job stopped making progress.

4. Cancellation is unreliable
   Cancel requests may not take effect, especially while a long Thunderbird API
   call or network request is active. Define cancellable stages and ensure the UI
   accurately distinguishes requested, acknowledged, and completed cancellation.
